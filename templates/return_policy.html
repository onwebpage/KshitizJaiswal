Return Policy

Information about product returns and exchanges

Last updated: October 15, 2025

Return Window

We offer refund/exchange within first 3 days from the date of your purchase. If 3 days have passed since your purchase, you will not be offered a return, exchange or refund of any kind.

Eligibility Criteria

In order to become eligible for a return or an exchange, the following conditions must be met:

Unused Condition

The purchased item should be unused and in the same condition as you received it

Original Packaging

The item must have original packaging intact

Sale Items

If the item was purchased on a sale, then it may not be eligible for a return/exchange

Replacements

Only such items are replaced by us (based on an exchange request), if such items are found defective or damaged.

Exempted Categories

You agree that there may be certain categories of products/items that are exempted from returns or refunds. Such categories of products would be identified to you at the time of purchase.

Return Process

For exchange/return accepted request(s) (as applicable):

1
Submit Request

Submit your return/exchange request through our customer service

2
Ship the Product

Once your returned product/item is received and inspected by us

3
Email Notification

We will send you an email to notify you about receipt of the returned/exchanged product

4
Quality Check

If approved after the quality check at our end, your request will be processed in accordance with our policies

Need Help?

If you have any questions about our return policy, please don't hesitate to contact us.
